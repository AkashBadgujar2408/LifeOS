# LifeOS user guide

LifeOS is one place for the whole of your life: what you want (goals), what you do (tasks, habits, calendar), what
you know (learning, documents), what you have (money, home, purchases), who you're close to (family), how you are
(health, journal), and the story all of that tells. This guide walks through it as someone using it, not building it.
For setup, see the [README](../README.md).

---

## Getting started

1. **Register** with a username, email and password. LifeOS then shows **eight recovery codes** — save them
   somewhere safe (a password manager or on paper). LifeOS sends no email, so a recovery code is the only way back in
   if you forget your password ("Forgot your password?" on the login page). Each code works once; **Settings › Recovery
   codes** makes a fresh set at any time.
2. Open **Settings › Time and money** and **Modules**:
   - **Time zone** — repeating events and reminders follow it.
   - **Base currency** (e.g. INR) — Finance adds everything up in it.
   - **Modules** — switch off what you don't use (Health, Career, Family…). They disappear from the sidebar, My Day,
     search, reports and achievements; your data stays and comes back when you switch them on again.
3. You land on **My Day**. Start by adding one goal, one habit and today's tasks — everything else can come later.

### Three things that work everywhere

| | |
|---|---|
| **Quick add** — the round <kbd>+</kbd> button or <kbd>Q</kbd> | Capture a task, note, journal line or idea in seconds. It goes to the **Inbox**, where you turn it into the real thing later (a task, a journal entry, a book to read…). |
| **Search** — <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> or <kbd>/</kbd> | Finds pages ("budgets", "net worth" — Enter opens it) and anything you've written: goals, tasks, events, journal days, contacts, transactions, books… |
| **Links** — "Linked items" on any item | Tie things together: a task to the goal it serves, a receipt to a purchase, a book to a skill. Links work both ways. |

The **sidebar** shows its groups as headings; click one to open its pages (one group at a time). The group of the
page you're on opens by itself, and a closed group still shows its counts (Inbox items, reminders due).

Dates are always written like **14/Oct/2026**; date fields accept that format or the calendar button next to them.

## My Day

Your daily page: today's date, tasks due, habits to tick, events, reminders that are due, documents expiring soon,
birthdays coming up, bills and renewals, and **Worth a look** — plain nudges like "your reading streak broke on
Saturday" or "Food has gone past its October budget". A nudge disappears by itself once you act on it; × hides it.
**Your figures** shows the numbers you choose to keep in view; **Previously on your story** is a short recap.

## Planning & growth

- **Goals** — long-term, short-term or bucket-list. Progress comes from one of: milestones you tick, a number you
  measure (pages read, km run — logged by hand or counted automatically from habits, study, tasks or workouts), a
  savings amount (from linked accounts), or a body target (Health). A goal's status (active, postponed, completed,
  abandoned) changes only with the status buttons, and every change is kept in its history. The **life vision** at the
  top of Goals is the "why" behind them.
- **Tasks** — Today, Overdue, Upcoming and Someday. Tasks can repeat, wait for other tasks, belong to a project, and
  carry a focus timer (the time you focus adds up as actual time spent).
- **Projects** — a group of tasks with a status.
- **Habits** — build habits (do something on chosen weekdays, optionally with a number like "20 pages") or avoid
  habits (days clean). Tick a day on the grid; streaks and consistency are worked out from what you ticked. Habits can
  have 7/30/90-day challenges and be grouped into morning/evening routines. The **library** has ready-made habits.

## Calendar & reminders

- **Calendar** — month/week/day views. Events can be all-day or timed and can repeat (daily, weekly, monthly,
  yearly). Dragging an event moves it; for a repeating one LifeOS asks whether to move just this one or every one.
  Task due dates, goal targets, birthdays, bills and renewals show up too, each in its own colour.
- **Reminders** — a reminder about anything (a task, a document, a person…), once or repeating. Due reminders show
  in the sidebar badge, on My Day and as a pop-up while LifeOS is open (LifeOS doesn't send notifications when it's
  closed).

## Journal

One entry per day (text, mood, photos, gratitude, lessons), a **weekly** and a **monthly reflection**, and
**Decisions** — write down a decision and when to look back at it; LifeOS reminds you on that day to note how it went.

## Learning

**Skills** (with a level from "Just starting" to "Could teach it" and its history), **Courses**, **Roadmaps** (steps
toward a skill), **Study** (a timer and study analytics), **Notes & review** (a knowledge base with spaced review) and
**Certifications** (with a renewal reminder). Books live in Leisure; a book read for a skill still counts toward it.

## Career

**Timeline** of roles (with promotions), **Salary** history, **Achievements** at work, **Applications** (a board by
stage; interview rounds go on the calendar), **Interview prep** (a question bank), **Mock interviews** (a record of
practice with an AI outside LifeOS), **Resumes** (versions with their files), **Reviews**, **Side income** and your
**Network** (people, longest out of touch first).

## Money

Everything is a **transaction** — income, expense or transfer between your accounts — so balances are always worked
out from what really happened, never typed in.

- **Accounts** — bank, cash, wallet, card; an account can hold several currencies.
- **Transactions** — the ledger, with categories as tags, receipts as attachments, and **Import** for a bank
  statement CSV (the import screen explains which files work and offers a sample).
- **Repeating** — rent, salary, SIPs: written into the ledger automatically when they fall due.
- **Budgets** — per category per month.
- **Net worth** — accounts + investments + money lent − loans still owed, over time.
- **Investments**, **Loans** (EMI or pay-as-you-go; what's owed comes from the actual payments), **Insurance** and
  **Taxes** (with reminders).
- **Forecast** — the money you already know is coming (repeating transactions, bills, EMIs, subscriptions…), and,
  with the AI assistant on, a reading of your past patterns (see below). **Plan** — savings and retirement
  projections.
- **Exchange rates** (Settings) — the rate of each other currency into your base currency, set by hand.

**Vouchers and gift cards:** a voucher that only works at one store (an Amazon shopping voucher, even if Amazon shows
it inside Amazon Pay) is a **reward** in Purchases with that store set — not money in a wallet account. When you shop
there, record the purchase and pick the voucher under "Rewards used"; the purchase amount is only what you paid
yourself. Only credit you can spend like the wallet's own money is "Wallet credit".

## Health (behind the vault)

Medical history, doctor visits, medicines and doses, exercise, sleep, body measurements, food and water, lab results,
wellbeing (stress/energy) and health goals. Everything written here is **encrypted**, and the Health pages ask for
your password (the **vault unlock**) before showing anything. Outside Health, LifeOS only ever says neutral things — a
doctor's visit on the calendar is "Doctor's appointment", never the reason. Settings shows a log of every time the
vault was opened.

## Home

Properties, **inventory** by room (with photos and bills), **vehicles** (PUC/registration reminders, a **fuel log**
that works out mileage), **warranties**, **maintenance** (with the next due date), utility **bills** (pay one and it
becomes a Finance expense) and household **projects** (budget vs what's been spent).

## Purchases

**Shopping lists**, a **wishlist**, purchase **history** (a purchase can create its expense, the inventory item and
its warranty in one go), **rewards** (vouchers, gift cards, coupons, cashback, points — with expiry reminders),
**subscriptions** (next charge and cost per month) and **returns** (with a return-by reminder). Deleting a purchase
gives back any voucher or points used on it.

## Family

People (everyone is one shared contact list), a **family tree**, birthdays and anniversaries (on the calendar and on
My Day a week ahead), **interactions** (who you saw or called), **keep in touch** (a gentle nudge when it's been a
while), **gift ideas** and shared **responsibilities**. Nothing here scores or ranks a person.

## Documents

Passports, IDs, certificates, policies — with their files, expiry dates and reminders. A **vault** document's files
are stored encrypted and open only after the unlock.

## Leisure

One backlog for books, films, series, games, albums and podcasts (Want → In progress → Finished), pages/episodes as
you go, ratings and reviews, highlights, **Year in media**, and **hobbies** with a practice timer.

## Your story

- **Life Story** — your life as a book: **chapters** you name, with what happened in each (goals passed, milestones,
  roles, books finished, journal highlights and photos, achievements) on the day it happened.
- **Missions** — your goals seen as missions (main, side, collectible), with prerequisites between them.
- **Life areas** — a level per area of life from what you've actually done, plus a monthly self-rating.
- **Achievements** — earned from real data on the day it happened (net worth ₹10 lakh, a book a month, a 30-day
  streak…). Nothing is lost for a missed day, there are no deadlines, and undoing what earned one takes it away.

## Reports

Goals, Habits, Productivity, Learning, Health, Money and Life balance over any range of days, each printable and
exportable as CSV, plus **custom reports** you can save, and the **Annual Life Review** for a whole year. A report's
figures always match the module's own page.

## The AI assistant (optional)

The assistant answers questions about your own data ("how much did I spend on food this month?"), creates and updates
things for you ("add a task to renew my passport next Friday" — it always shows a card to **Confirm** first, and it
can never delete anything), narrates your story, and reads patterns for the Forecast tab. It uses **your own Mistral
API key**; nothing is sent anywhere until you add one and switch the assistant on.

**Getting a key (free, about 5 minutes):**

1. Open Mistral's console (<https://console.mistral.ai>) and sign up or sign in.
2. If it asks for a plan, pick the **free** one — no card needed. Mistral may ask you to verify your phone number.
3. In the left sidebar choose **API Keys** (<https://console.mistral.ai/api-keys>), then **Create new key**.
4. Name it (e.g. "LifeOS") and pick an expiry date. If it asks about connectors, **Shared connectors only** is fine.
   Press **Create new key**.
5. **Copy the key straight away** — Mistral shows it only once. If you lose it, create another.
6. In LifeOS, **Settings › AI assistant**: paste it into **Your Mistral API key**, press **Save key**, then **Test
   key**, and switch the assistant on.

Good to know: the key is stored encrypted and never leaves your server except to call Mistral; what you ask counts
against your Mistral plan (the free plan has rate limits); on the free plan Mistral's terms may let them use what's
sent to improve their models — under every answer, **What was sent** shows exactly what left LifeOS. Health data is
only ever sent while the vault is unlocked, and every time it is, it's logged.

## Your data

- **Download my data** (Settings › Your data) gives a zip with everything — JSON, a CSV per table and your files
  (vault files and health notes decrypted, so keep it somewhere safe; it asks for the vault unlock when there's
  something sensitive in it). It can be **imported into a new, empty account**, on this machine or another one.
- **Delete my account** erases everything for good.
- Whoever runs the server should back up the database **and** the files and keys folders together (see the README) —
  without the keys folder, encrypted files and notes can't be read.

## Accessibility

LifeOS works with the keyboard alone (Tab, Enter, Esc; arrow keys in pickers), every field has a visible label or an
accessible name, text meets WCAG AA contrast, and every page works at phone width.

## When something looks wrong

- **"Can't reach the LifeOS server"** — the server isn't running; whoever runs it needs to start it.
- **Sent back to the login page** — a login lasts 24 hours; log in again and you return where you were.
- **A Health page keeps asking for the password** — the unlock lasts a while, then locks again on purpose.
- **A number looks wrong** — every figure in LifeOS is worked out from your entries, never stored, so the fix is
  always in the entries themselves (a missing transaction, a habit day not ticked, an exchange rate not set).
